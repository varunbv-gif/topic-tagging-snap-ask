Every conversation, placed on the topic tree

271 tutoring conversations from one day, each tagged to a single subject, chapter and exact topic node. Tagged from nine input parameters: who the student is, what they sent, what the bot answered, the bot's own coverage notes, and the grade, board and target on their profile.

Subject, chapter and topic are copied exactly from topictree; the tree column names which of the 73 trees carries that chapter. Confidence is HIGH where the bot's own note states the chapter's content outright, MED where it was inferred from worked examples or the student's words, LOW where it is a guess.

Grade, board and target come from the central student profile, joined on user_id. They rank the candidate trees but never restrict them — 14.5% of these students were working above or below their own grade.
