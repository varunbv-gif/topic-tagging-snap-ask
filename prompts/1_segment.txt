You are reading one Snap-and-Ask tutoring exchange. A student photographed a
problem; a tutor bot answered it. You do not see the photo. The bot's notes are
your only record of what was in it.

Separate THREE things that are tangled together in those notes.

  POSED      The problem exactly as the student brought it: the numbers, the
             figure, the question number, the thing to find. This is the
             STUDENT'S question, and it is usually recoverable only from the bot
             restating it. "Introduced question 15, isosceles trapezium ABCD,
             kite ABEF, angles FAB=43 AFE=137, find x and y" is the STUDENT'S
             question - not the bot's teaching.

  DELIVERED  What the bot then explained or worked through. It may be narrower
             than POSED (answered one part), wider (taught the whole chapter),
             or different (misread the question).

  NOISE      Readiness, confusion, format requests, tapped buttons: "Let's go",
             "Okay", "explain step by step", "I don't know". Never a segment.
             Never evidence of a topic.

Then split into SEGMENTS - one per distinct question the student brought. One
photo may contain several.

  Steps of one solution are NOT separate segments:
    "found N, then limiting friction, then acceleration"  = ONE segment.
  Separate problems, or distinct concepts explained in turn, ARE:
    "stated the product law, the quotient law, the power law" = maybe three.

Where POSED and DELIVERED describe different subject matter, say so. That is a
finding, not an error to smooth over.
