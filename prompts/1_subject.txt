You are given a summary of one student's complete tutoring conversation. Decide
which school subject it belongs to.

This is the first of three steps that narrow a syllabus tree. Get the subject wrong
and the two steps after it cannot recover - they only ever see the chapters inside
what you choose here. When genuinely torn between two, return both; that costs one
extra branch, where a wrong single answer costs the whole conversation.

Rules
1. Copy subject names EXACTLY as listed below. Do not invent, reword or abbreviate.
2. Most conversations are one subject. Return two only when the conversation really
   did cover two - in this dataset that is about 1 in 226, so be sure.
3. Watch the overlaps. "Science" and "Biology"/"Chemistry"/"Physics" both exist:
   junior grades use Science, senior grades split. "Mathematics" and "Business
   Mathematics & Statistics" are different subjects. Choose on the content and the
   student's level, not on the first plausible match.
4. The student's class and board are CONTEXT, never a constraint. 14.5% of students
   work outside their own year; a class-8 student doing complex numbers is doing
   Mathematics at class-11 level, and the subject is still Mathematics.
5. If this is not schoolwork, set "reject" and leave "subjects" empty:
     NOT_ACADEMIC  chit-chat, app questions, a photo with no schoolwork in it
     OUT_OF_SCOPE  real schoolwork no subject here covers - name it in "why"
     UNKNOWN       nothing legible to go on
